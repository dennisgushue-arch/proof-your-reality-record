Data Deletion Request

Effective date: May 24, 2026

Public URL: https://proofrealityrecord.xyz/legal/data-deletion.html

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at privacy@proofrealityrecord.xyz.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Email from the account-associated email address.
  2. Subject line: Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your account email and confirmation you want deletion.

What We Delete

We delete or anonymize account profile, incidents, evidence metadata, and related user-generated records, subject to legal obligations.

Timing

Requests are typically processed within 30 days, unless legal or security obligations require retention.

Verification

We may request additional information to verify request ownership and protect account security.
