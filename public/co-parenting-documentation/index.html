How to Document Co-Parenting Incidents Clearly

Ongoing co-parenting disputes can become difficult to remember accurately over time. A clear record helps you preserve dates, messages, incidents, and context while the details are still fresh.

What to record

  • Date and time of the incident
  • Where it happened
  • Who was involved
  • What was said or done
  • Relevant messages, screenshots, photos, or files
  • Any witnesses or follow-up actions

Keep facts separate from interpretation

  • Write down what happened in specific, neutral language.
  • Avoid exaggeration, assumptions, or emotional conclusions.
  • If you include your interpretation, label it separately from the factual record.

Build a timeline over time

  • Repeated issues are easier to understand when incidents are organized chronologically.
  • A timeline can help you spot recurring patterns and keep older events from getting lost.
