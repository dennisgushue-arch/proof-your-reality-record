How to Document Incidents Properly

Good incident documentation is specific, timely, and organized. The goal is to create a record you can understand later without relying on memory alone.

Record incidents as soon as possible

  • Capture the date and time
  • Describe what happened in plain language
  • Identify people involved
  • Add supporting evidence
  • Note what happened immediately afterward

Be factual and specific

  • Use exact details instead of vague summaries.
  • Keep direct quotes separate from paraphrased statements.

Organize the history

  • A chronological timeline makes repeated incidents easier to review.
  • Keep related evidence connected to the incident it supports.

Keep the record organized while the details are still fresh.
