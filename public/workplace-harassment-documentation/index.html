How to Document Workplace Harassment

Learn what to record after a workplace harassment incident, how to preserve emails and messages, and how to organize a clear incident timeline.

What should I record after a workplace incident?

  • Date, time, and location
  • Who was involved and who witnessed the event
  • What was said or done
  • Relevant emails, chat messages, photos, or documents
  • Any report made to a supervisor or HR
  • What response you received

Should I write down exact quotes?

  • Use exact quotes when you clearly remember them.
  • If you are paraphrasing, say so.
  • Avoid presenting uncertain wording as an exact quotation.

How should I organize emails and screenshots?

  • Keep original files whenever possible.
  • Connect each item to the incident it relates to.
  • Use consistent dates and labels.

How often should I update my workplace incident log?

  • Update it as soon as practical after an important incident.
  • Add follow-up actions as they occur.

Why is chronology important?

  • Chronology can show what happened before and after a report.
  • It helps connect repeated behavior, escalation, or follow-up actions.
