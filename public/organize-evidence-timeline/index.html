How to Organize Evidence and Build a Clear Timeline

Screenshots, emails, notes, photos, and documents become much more useful when they are connected to specific events and arranged in chronological order.

Group evidence by incident

  • Attach messages, screenshots, photos, or documents to the event they relate to.
  • Avoid keeping everything in one unstructured folder.

Use consistent dates and labels

  • Label incidents clearly.
  • Use accurate dates and times whenever possible.
  • Keep descriptions concise and searchable.

Review the timeline regularly

  • Chronological organization helps reveal patterns and missing details.
  • A consistent timeline is easier to understand than scattered records.

Keep the record organized while the details are still fresh.
