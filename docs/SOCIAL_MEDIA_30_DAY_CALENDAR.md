## Hook Bank for Short Videos (A/B Variants)

Use these A/B options in the first 1.5 seconds.

1. **Memory fades fast**
   - A: “Memory fades fast after stressful moments.”
   - B: “If it matters later, document it now.”
2. **Facts not vibes**
   - A: “POV: everyone remembers it differently.”
   - B: “Facts, not vibes—here’s my workflow.”
3. **3 mistakes after incidents**
   - A: “3 mistakes that weaken your record 👇”
   - B: “Don’t make these 3 incident-documentation mistakes.”
4. **Prepare Me spotlight**
   - A: “Before difficult conversations, I do this 1-minute prep.”
   - B: “Need to stay calm before a high-pressure interaction?”
5. **Capture workflow**
   - A: “One place for notes, evidence, and timeline.”
   - B: “Capture now. Clarify later.”
6. **Timeline clarity**
   - A: “Your timeline should stay clear—even under stress.”
   - B: “Proof beats panic.”
7. **Evidence organization**
   - A: “Scattered screenshots = lost context.”
   - B: “Organized evidence changes everything.”
8. **Neutral summaries**
   - A: “Separate facts from assumptions in seconds.”
   - B: “Neutral summaries help you stay consistent.”
9. **Export packet value**
   - A: “Need a clean record? Export this.”
   - B: “Build your packet before you need it.”
10. **Privacy-first angle**
    - A: “Private-first documentation for real-life incidents.”
    - B: “Document clearly, without broadcasting your business.”

## Weekly Optimization Rules

- Keep **one core variable** per A/B test (hook line only).
- Choose winners by:
  - 3-second hold rate
  - average watch percentage
  - saves + shares per 1,000 views
- Re-cut winners in Week 4 with:
  - faster first frame
  - clearer text overlay
  - stronger single CTA

---

## Creative Notes for Production Team

- 9:16 framing, captions burned in, first screen should show product UI quickly.
- Keep most shorts in the **12–25 second** range.
- Use calm, neutral language (avoid fear framing).
- Include this when relevant: **“Not legal advice. Documentation support only.”**
