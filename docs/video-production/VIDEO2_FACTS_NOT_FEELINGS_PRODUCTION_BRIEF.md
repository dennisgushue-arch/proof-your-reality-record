Brand: **Proof Your Reality Record**  
Format: **9:16 vertical (1080x1920)**  
Length: **30 seconds**  
Primary objective: **Show how structured documentation reduces confusion and contradiction**  
Creative approach: **Cinematic motion-graphics ad (no talking head)**

- Frame rate: **30 fps**
- Captions: **burned in**, high-contrast
- Text safe zone: center **80%**

- Compliance footer (optional end card): **“Not legal advice. Documentation support only.”**

## Motion-Graphics Storyboard + Camera Direction

### Scene 1 (0:00–0:04)

- **Visual:** Two mirrored panels replaying conflicting text snippets from the same moment.
- **Camera:** Wide-to-medium push-in with slight lateral drift.
- **UI/FX:** Text divergence animation (same sentence morphs into two versions).
- **Edit note:** establish conflict fast; headline hits by 0:00.4.

### Scene 2 (0:04–0:09)

- **Visual:** “Fact” and “Assumption” cards collide in a dark volumetric space.
- **Camera:** Orbit arc with shallow DOF and subtle camera shake.
- **UI/FX:** Assumption cards glow red then fade; fact cards remain crisp.
- **Edit note:** use controlled chaos, then decelerate before Scene 3.

### Scene 3 (0:09–0:15)

- **Visual:** Premium animated Proof UI appears; **Start Live Incident** tap pulse triggers form fields.
- **Camera:** Top-down glide to frontal hero angle.
- **UI/FX:** Fact fields populate in sequence: who → where → when.
- **Edit note:** transition from confusion to clarity.

### Scene 4 (0:15–0:23)

- **Visual:** Photos, voice note waveform, and notes cards slide into a central timeline rail.
- **Camera:** Lateral tracking shot with layered parallax depth.
- **UI/FX:** Timestamp chips auto-assign and lock.
- **Edit note:** keep UI legible with clean spacing and measured timing.

### Scene 5 (0:23–0:30)

- **Visual:** Dashboard hero frame with contradiction indicator and stable timeline grid.
- **Camera:** Slow crane-down + micro push-in to final lock frame.
- **UI/FX:** Contradiction alert pulse in restrained amber; background noise decreases.
- **Edit note:** hold final text for 1.8–2.0 seconds.

## On-Screen Text Styling

- Typeface: bold geometric sans (or your existing brand type)

- Body caption size: 58–72 px equivalent

## Visual Design System (Cinematic Premium)

- Background palette: charcoal, midnight blue, graphite gradients
- Highlight palette: restrained cyan/teal + warm amber alert accent
- Surface treatment: soft grain, low-opacity noise, glass card edges
