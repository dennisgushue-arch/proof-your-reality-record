## Motion-Graphics Storyboard + Camera Direction

### Scene 1 (0:00–0:03)

- **Visual:** Dark abstract environment with floating message fragments fading and drifting out of focus.
- **Camera:** 35mm virtual lens, slow dolly-in with shallow depth-of-field simulation.
- **UI/FX:** Fragmented chat bubbles dissolve into dust particles.
- **Edit note:** hard fade from black; headline appears in first 0.3s with cinematic impact.

### Scene 2 (0:03–0:08)

- **Visual:** Stylized 3D phone mockup rotating in space, camera roll cards stack and bury each other rapidly.
- **Camera:** orbit-left + push-in combo; slight handheld noise layer for urgency.
- **UI/FX:** Screenshot cards use depth layers and motion blur streaks.
- **Edit note:** use time-compression effect and vignette to communicate “details fading.”

### Scene 3 (0:08–0:14)

- **Visual:** Premium animated Proof app mockup appears on luminous dark surface.
- **Camera:** top-down glide to front-perspective reveal.
- **UI/FX:** Tap pulse on **Start Live Incident** with radial glow and micro-particle burst.
- **Edit note:** ease into calm, controlled pacing to signal clarity.

### Scene 4 (0:14–0:22)

- **Visual:** Modular UI cards (Photo, Voice, Notes, Timeline) animate into one central timeline rail.
- **Camera:** slow lateral track with layered parallax.
- **UI/FX:** Timestamp chips auto-populate; waveform animates for voice note.
- **Edit note:** 2–3 crisp inserts, then settle on organized timeline composition.

### Scene 5 (0:22–0:30)

- **Visual:** Dashboard hero shot in dark premium UI shell; contradiction alert appears as refined signal card.
- **Camera:** smooth crane-down into locked center frame.
- **UI/FX:** alert pulse (subtle amber), timeline lines stabilize and brighten.
- **Edit note:** hold final frame 1.8–2.0s for message retention.

## On-Screen Text Styling

- Typeface: bold geometric sans (or your existing brand type)
- Hook line size: 110–140 px equivalent at 1080x1920
- Body caption size: 58–72 px equivalent
- Color: white text with soft shadow for readability on dark backgrounds
- Accent color: app brand accent for key terms (e.g., “Live Incident”)
- Text animation: subtle fade-up + tracking-in (avoid bounce effects)

---

## Visual Design System (Cinematic Premium)

- Background palette: charcoal, midnight blue, graphite gradients
- Highlight palette: restrained cyan/teal + warm amber alert accent
- Surface treatment: soft grain, low-opacity noise, glass card edges
- Lighting: top-edge rim light and center vignette for depth
- Do not use: emoji stickers, playful pop effects, saturated neon overload

---

## Audio Direction

- VO type: **professional studio voiceover** (neutral, trustworthy, authoritative)
- VO tone: calm, direct, confident (not dramatic)
- Bed music: **cinematic technology bed** (minimal pulse, modern ambient)
- SFX: subtle UI clicks, soft whooshes, card settles, gentle low-end impacts
- Keep VO intelligibility above all else
- Loudness target: social-friendly mix with clean dialogue priority
