# Social Media Marketing Pack (Instagram + TikTok)

Brand: **Proof Your Reality Record**  
Positioning: **Private-first incident and evidence journaling app for factual timelines**  
Core promise: **Capture details while they’re fresh, organize evidence, and prepare a neutral record.**

---

## Audience Segments

1. **Everyday users** who need better records after difficult incidents.
2. **Safety-conscious users** who want private-by-default documentation.
3. **Creators/professionals** who value clean timelines, structure, and export-ready packets.

---

## Voice & Tone Guidelines

- Calm, clear, supportive.
- Empowering, not fear-based.
- Privacy-forward language.
- Avoid legal guarantees or outcomes.

Use this compliance line where relevant:  
**“Proof Your Reality Record helps you document events; it is not legal advice.”**

## Creative Production Checklist

- Use vertical 9:16 framing for all short-form videos.
- Keep opening hook within first 1.5 seconds.
- Add burned-in captions for silent viewers.
- Show real product screens early (first 3 seconds).
- End with one clear CTA (save, follow, comment, DM).

## Performance Targets (Initial)

Adjust creative weekly based on top hooks, watch-time drop points, and save/share ratio.
